# Summarize — what a recording says and shows (`/media summarize <file>`)

1. Follow [Read the recording](../../SKILL.md#read-the-recording).
2. `Write` `summary.md` into the capture folder:

```markdown
# <a title that names the topic, not the file>

<source path> · <duration mm:ss> · spoken in <language>

## TL;DR
<two or three sentences>

## Timeline
| Time | Said | Shown |
|---|---|---|
| 00:12 | … | … (frames/<file>.png) |

## Key points
- …

## Open questions
- <only what the recording leaves unanswered; omit the section when nothing is>
```

The timeline keeps one row per real step or topic change, not one per transcript line. An audio file drops the Shown column.

## Reply

The TL;DR, the number of key points, and the path to `summary.md`.
