## 🎬 Media: what I can do

Give me a video or audio file on this computer (a path, or drag it in). The speech is transcribed on this Mac and never uploaded. Needs `yap` (`brew install yap`, macOS 26+) for speech and `ffmpeg` (`brew install ffmpeg`) for screenshots.

| Ask | Playbook | You get |
|---|---|---|
| "What's in this video?", `/media summarize` | **summarize** | A short summary, a timeline of what was said and shown, and the key points |
| "The ticket has a video", `/media ticket` | **ticket** | Steps to reproduce, expected vs actual, on-screen errors, and questions for the reporter |
| "Transcribe this", `/media transcribe` | **transcribe** | The transcript with a timestamp on every line |
| "Screenshot at 1:13", `/media screenshots` | **screenshots** | Still images at scene changes, at set times, or every few seconds |
| "Remember this meeting", `/media remember` | **remember** | The summary and transcript saved to the brain for the next compile |
