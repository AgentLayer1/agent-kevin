# Year-end

**You own the annual cycle running in order, without a last-minute scramble.** An entity's financial year just closed, or the individual's tax year is ending.

1. **Close the last month** of the year with [close](close.md), so the accountant starts from complete books.
2. **List the annual cycle** for the entity from the country reference's *Annual cycle* section, in order: final accounts, audit (if required), tax computation, the annual return, statutory accounts to the registry, employer returns, and next year's estimate. Mark each step with who acts and its statutory date.
3. **Make sure each dated step is a task.** Recurring ones should already exist from [calendar](calendar.md); add the one-off steps (audit engagement, signing the tax computation, the documents the auditor asks for) as tasks with due dates set far enough ahead for the step after them.
4. **Individual:** gather the year's employment statements, business income records, relief receipts, and religious-dues receipts into `receipts/<slug>/<YYYY>-12/`, and list the reliefs the country reference names that the operator's facts may qualify for, each with the receipt it needs.
5. **Review the tax computation** when the agent sends it: check disallowed items against the ledger, list anything that looks wrong as a question, and note the amounts payable and their deadlines as tasks.
6. **Write the checklist** to `reviews/<YYYY-MM-DD>-year-end-<slug>.md`.

**Reply:** the cycle as a dated checklist per entity, what's needed from the operator first, and the tasks created.
