## 🌱 Seed: what I can do

Fork this agent's persona and setup into a teammate's home, or take one on, without anything personal crossing over.

| Ask | Playbook | You get |
|---|---|---|
| "Export the agent for Ada", "make a seed bundle", `/seed export` | **export** | An interview that picks what to share, a per-file review, then the zip and the one line your teammate runs |
| "Import this seed", a `*-seed.zip`, `/seed import <path>` | **import** | A dry-run plan, your call on each conflict, the overlay, and the list of keys to fill in |
