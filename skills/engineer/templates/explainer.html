    How stock reservations work

    Placing an order holds its units in a reservations table instead of decrementing stock at payment, so two buyers can't both pay for the last unit. This page walks the flow, the decisions behind it, and what is still open.

    The short version

    One table owns availability. available = stock − active reservations, read under a row lock. Payment confirms a reservation; cancel or expiry releases it.

    How an order flows

    An order reserves on place, confirms on payment and releases on cancel. Every step takes the product row lock first, so two orders for the last unit serialize.

    sequenceDiagram
  autonumber
  participant B as Buyer
  participant O as OrderService
  participant R as reservations
  B->>O: place order (2 units)
  O->>R: lock product, insert hold
  R-->>O: held until now + 15 min
  B->>O: pay
  O->>R: confirm hold
  Note over O,R: cancel or expiry deletes the hold

    The lock is the product row's, taken before stock is read.

    Before and after

    Before

    Stock decremented at payment. Two buyers could both pass the check and pay for one unit.

    After

    Stock is held at placement under a lock. The second buyer sees it gone before paying.
