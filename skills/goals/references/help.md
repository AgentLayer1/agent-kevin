## 🎯 Goals: what I can do

What should be true by tonight, this week, this month and this year. The goals live in your task hub; focus turns them into tasks.

| Ask | Playbook | You get |
|---|---|---|
| "Set today's goals", `/goals day` | **day** | One to three outcomes for today, scored in the evening wrap |
| "Plan the week", `/goals week` | **week** | Monday: two or three week goals, planned and written; `/goals week interview` (or "set my weekly goals") grills you first. Friday: done, partial or missed, and where the rest goes |
| "Monthly goals", "this month's themes", `/goals month` | **month** | Two to four themes with a success check, and a report card on last month |
| "Set this year's goals", `/goals year` | **year** | Outcomes per quarter with an end-of-quarter check, past quarters marked landed or missed |

A bare `/goals` runs whichever horizon is due, or the day when nothing is.
