## 🎯 Attention: what I can do

Your day in one place: what today is for, what slipped, the week and month, the roadmap in flight, who's waiting on you, and where every session left off. The home view is the dashboard's Today tab; a project can have its own page.

### 🗓 The day

| Ask | Playbook | You get |
|---|---|---|
| `/attention`, "what's waiting on me?" | **refresh** | Your PRs, the reviews you owe and (with Slack) the replies you owe, pulled fresh, and a one-glance card |
| "Plan my day", `/attention plan` | **plan** | Today cut to three, each with its reason; carried work settled; a task offered for every roadmap milestone nothing moves |
| "I'm overwhelmed", "get organized", `/attention organize` | **organize** | Everything in your head sorted into tasks and notes, the gaps asked about (dates, week goals, overdue), today cut to three, a first move, and what's parked |
| "Take this on", a pasted line, list, or link | **add** | A planned task after one question, never a duplicate |

### 🎤 The room

| Ask | Playbook | You get |
|---|---|---|
| "Standup is coming up", "what did I do yesterday?", `/attention standup 48` | **standup** | Did / next / blocked from git, PRs, sessions and the board, a glance card to read while talking, with the full update and its evidence saved as a report |

### 🛰️ Sessions

| Ask | Playbook | You get |
|---|---|---|
| "Checkpoint this session", "write a handoff" | **checkpoint** | This session as a checklist: done, now, next, and what's waiting on you; filed into knowledge when it ends |
| "What was I working on?", "which session needs me?", `/attention checkpoint all 48` | **checkpoint all** | The same checklist for every session from the last 24h: the decisions waiting on you first, ranked, each card with its resume command, then an offer to jump to one |

### 💬 With Slack connected

| Ask | Playbook | You get |
|---|---|---|
| First refresh | **backfill** | The priorities already posted in Slack, triaged into tasks you confirm |

Add a project to scope any of the day's playbooks: `/attention plan acme`. Goals for the day, week, month and year live in `/goals`.
