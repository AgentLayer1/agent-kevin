## 🌅 Briefing: what I can do

The day's bookends and a quick check, read from your tasks, sessions, git and goals.

| Ask | Playbook | You get |
|---|---|---|
| "Brief me", "morning brief", `/briefing morning` | **morning** | Today's priorities, what moved overnight, goals, projects, stale work, the news that touches your work, and one first move |
| "Wrap up the day", "evening wrap", `/briefing evening` | **evening** | What shipped, drafted and stalled today, how today's goals landed, and tomorrow's first move |
| "Quick pulse", "anything on fire?", `/briefing pulse` | **pulse** | Anything overdue, stale or blocked, or a one-line all clear |

A bare `/briefing` picks morning until one has run today (3am to 9pm), then evening.
