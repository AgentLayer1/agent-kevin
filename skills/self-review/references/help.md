## 🧹 Self-review: what I can do

Once a month I clean up what I know and how I work, with you deciding. Sync asks when it's due.

| Ask | Playbook | You get |
|---|---|---|
| "Clean up the brain", "what's gone stale", `/self-review brain` | **brain** | Questions about stale tasks, quiet projects, outdated memory lines, decisions worth keeping, old articles and captures, four at a time with my recommendation first. Each answer is applied as you give it |
| "Review your own instructions", `/self-review rules` | **rules** | Your corrections turned into better rules: what to prune, add, move or enforce, and what to promote to the plugin |
| "Run a self-review", `/self-review` | **brain**, then **rules** | Both in one sitting, with one report |

Nothing is deleted without your yes, and every deleted line is quoted in the report.
