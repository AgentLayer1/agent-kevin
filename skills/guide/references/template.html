    Your first week at Acme

    Everything you need to go from a fresh laptop to answering customers with your agent beside you. Each section is short, the steps are in order, and nothing assumes you've done this before.

    15-minute read Setup takes about an hour Tick the boxes as you go
    00

    Who does what

    Three players. People decide; the agent does the legwork.

    You

    The voice of Acme
    • Read the agent's drafts, edit and send them
    • Handle anything sensitive yourself

    Your agent

    Your assistant
    • Sorts every new request
    • Writes a first draft of each reply
    • Turns real problems into cards for the team

    The product team

    The people who build Acme
    • Decide what each card needs
    • Fix it and ship it
    The one promise The agent never talks to customers. Every reply goes out only after you read it and press send.

    Your routine

    Once a day, about twenty minutes.

    1. Ask your agent to check the inboxIt sorts what's new and drafts the replies.
    2. Send the draftsEdit each one, send it, or delete it and write your own.

    Things you can say to your agent

    "Check the inbox"The full run.
    "Make that shorter"Rewrites the draft in front of you.

    The card tags

    Each card wears one tag that says where it is.

    TagWhat it meansWhat you do
    newNobody has looked yet.Nothing.
    needs infoThe team needs more detail.Ask the customer.
    acceptedIt will be fixed.Nothing.

    Ground rules

    A few lines that keep customers safe.

    You press sendThe agent only writes private drafts.
    No personal details on cardsCards link to the request and nothing more.

    Questions you might have

    What if a draft is wrong?
