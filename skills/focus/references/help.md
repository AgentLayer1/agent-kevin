## 🎯 Focus: what I can do

Your day in one place: what today is for, what slipped, the week and month, the roadmap in flight, who's waiting on you, and where every session left off. The home view is the dashboard's Today tab; a project can have its own page.

### 🗓 The day

| Ask | Playbook | You get |
|---|---|---|
| `/focus`, "what's waiting on me?" | **refresh** | Your PRs, the reviews you owe and (with Slack) the replies you owe, pulled fresh, and a one-glance card |
| "Plan my day", `/focus plan` | **plan** | Today cut to three, each with its reason; carried work settled; a task offered for every roadmap milestone nothing moves |
| "I'm overwhelmed", "get organized", `/focus organize` | **organize** | Everything in your head sorted into tasks and notes, the gaps asked about (dates, week goals, overdue), today cut to three, a first move, and what's parked |
| "Take this on", a pasted line, list, or link | **add** | A planned task after one question, never a duplicate |

### 🎤 The room

| Ask | Playbook | You get |
|---|---|---|
| "Standup is coming up", "what did I do yesterday?", `/focus standup 48` | **standup** | Did / next / blocked from git, PRs, sessions and the board, a card to read while talking, and the evidence below it |

### 🛰️ Sessions

| Ask | Playbook | You get |
|---|---|---|
| "Where am I?", "what was I working on?", `/focus where-am-i 48` | **where-am-i** | Every session from the last 24h: what it was about, where it stands, and its resume command |
| "Which session needs me?", `/focus triage` | **triage** | The sessions waiting on you, ranked, and the one to resume |
| "Checkpoint this session", "write a handoff" | **checkpoint** | A short pickup note for this session, filed into knowledge when it ends |

### 💬 With Slack connected

| Ask | Playbook | You get |
|---|---|---|
| First refresh | **backfill** | The priorities already posted in Slack, triaged into tasks you confirm |

Add a project to scope any of the day's playbooks: `/focus plan acme`. Goals for the day, week, month and year live in `/goals`.
